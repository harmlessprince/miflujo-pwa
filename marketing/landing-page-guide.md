# MiFlujo Landing Page Guide

Launch goal: convert interested visitors into early-access waitlist users, with a special push to recruit 10 free trial users who can provide feedback and testimonials.

Primary CTA:

> Join early access

Secondary CTA:

> DM "MiFlujo"

B2B CTA:

> Request a demo

## Recommended Page Structure

Build one focused landing page first. Add separate pages later when the product link, pricing, privacy policy, and business demo flow are ready.

Recommended route:

- `/` or `/early-access`: public landing page.
- `/privacy`: privacy and data-use page.
- `/terms`: terms page.
- `/demo`: optional B2B demo request page.

## Landing Page Sections

### 1. Hero

Purpose: create immediate tension and explain the product in one glance.

Headline options:

- Your bank statement knows more than your balance.
- Turn bank statements into financial intelligence.
- Understand the full story behind your money.

Recommended hero copy:

> MiFlujo helps individuals, businesses, lenders, and auditors turn Nigerian bank statements into spending insights, cashflow analysis, recurring payment detection, unusual transaction flags, and AI-powered answers.

Primary CTA:

> Join early access

Secondary CTA:

> See how it works

Trust note:

> We do not ask for your bank login credentials.

### 2. Social Proof / Early Access Strip

Purpose: make the launch feel active and limited.

Copy:

> We are selecting 10 early users for free trial access before public launch.

Supporting text:

> Early users will help shape MiFlujo and may be invited to share feedback or a testimonial.

CTA:

> Apply for free trial access

### 3. Problem Section

Purpose: show the pain before showing features.

Headline:

> Bank statements are full of answers, but painful to read manually.

Pain points:

- Individuals struggle to see where money really goes.
- SMEs need clearer cashflow visibility.
- Lenders spend time reviewing statement behavior.
- Auditors and accountants need faster transaction pattern review.
- Raw statements are noisy, repetitive, and hard to compare.

### 4. How It Works

Purpose: make the product feel simple.

Use 3 steps:

1. Upload your statement
   - PDF, CSV, or XLSX.
   - Support starts with major Nigerian banks and wallets.

2. MiFlujo analyzes the data
   - Parses transactions, categories, merchants, income, expenses, and cashflow.

3. Get financial intelligence
   - See insights, ask AI questions, review recurring payments, and spot unusual transactions.

CTA after section:

> Join the waitlist

### 5. Product Preview

Purpose: show that this is real.

Recommended snippets:

- Upload statement screen.
- Statement processed success screen.
- Dashboard overview.
- Spending insights page.
- AI assistant page.
- Transaction list/detail page.

Important:

- Blur account numbers, names, balances, and transaction descriptions.
- Use sample data where possible.
- Keep screenshots cropped around one feature per image.

### 6. Core Features

Purpose: convert product capability into user benefit.

Feature cards:

- Statement parsing
  - Upload supported bank or wallet statements and convert them into structured transaction data.

- Spending insights
  - Understand where money goes by category, merchant, and date range.

- Cashflow analysis
  - See income, spending, net cashflow, and month-over-month changes.

- Recurring payment detection
  - Identify repeated payments, subscriptions, obligations, and predictable outflows.

- Unusual transaction flags
  - Surface transactions and spending spikes that may need review.

- AI-powered questions
  - Ask questions like "Why did my balance drop?" or "What did I spend most on?"

- Export and reporting
  - Review normalized statement data and summary reports.

### 7. Audience Section

Purpose: handle the broad market without confusing the visitor.

Headline:

> Built for anyone who works closely with bank statements.

Cards:

- Individuals
  - Understand spending, recurring payments, and cashflow.

- SMEs and founders
  - Get clearer visibility into business money movement.

- Lenders
  - Review statement behavior and cashflow patterns faster.

- Auditors and accountants
  - Organize transaction patterns, categories, and unusual activity.

CTA options:

- Individuals/SMEs: Join early access.
- Lenders/auditors: Request a demo.

### 8. Trust And Data Use

Purpose: reduce fear around bank statement uploads.

Headline:

> Built with privacy in mind.

Copy:

> MiFlujo uses your bank statement to extract transactions, summarize cashflow, and generate spending insights. You can delete processed statement data from your account.

Trust bullets:

- We do not ask for your bank login credentials.
- Statements are used to generate your financial insights.
- Processed statement data can be deleted from your account.
- Sensitive demo screenshots should use sample or blurred data.

CTA:

> Read our privacy notes

Link target:

> `/privacy`

### 9. Testimonials

Purpose: add proof after the first 10 early users test the product.

Before testimonials are available, use this section:

Headline:

> Early users are testing MiFlujo now.

Copy:

> We are working with the first 10 users to learn what MiFlujo helps them discover from their statements.

After testimonials are available, collect:

- Name or initials.
- User type: individual, SME, lender, auditor.
- What MiFlujo helped them understand.
- One short quote.
- Permission to publish.

Good testimonial prompts:

- What did MiFlujo help you understand?
- What surprised you from your statement?
- Was it easier than reviewing the statement manually?
- Would you recommend it to someone else?

Headline:

CTA:

## CTA Strategy

Use no more than three CTA labels across the landing page:

- Join early access
- Request a demo
- See how it works

Primary CTA behavior:

- If the product link is not ready, open a waitlist form.
- If no form is ready, use a mailto link or WhatsApp/DM instruction temporarily.

Recommended waitlist form fields:

- Name
- Email
- WhatsApp number
- User type: individual, business, lender, auditor, accountant, other
- What do you want to understand from bank statements?
- Can we contact you for feedback and a possible testimonial?

Recommended demo form fields:

- Name
- Work email
- Organization
- Role
- Use case: lending, audit, accounting, SME finance, other
- Number of statements reviewed monthly
- Preferred contact method

## Landing Page Design Notes

- Show the product clearly in the first viewport.
- Use real product snippets with sample or blurred financial data.
- Avoid a generic fintech hero with only abstract shapes.
- Keep the first screen focused on one promise and one CTA.
- Use the existing MiFlujo logo and app visual language.
- Make sections scannable with short copy and clear feature cards.
- Keep trust language visible near the upload/product preview sections.

## Launch Page Checklist

- Hero has clear product promise.
- Primary CTA works.
- Waitlist form is connected.
- Product snippets use safe sample or blurred data.
- Trust/data-use copy is visible.
- Audience section covers individuals, SMEs, lenders, and auditors.
- Demo CTA exists for B2B users.
- Privacy and terms links exist, even if basic.
- Testimonials section can be updated after first 10 users.
- Mobile layout is checked.
