# MiFlujo Social Media Launch Kit

Launch window: End of June 2026.

Primary campaign line:

> Your bank statement knows more than your balance. MiFlujo helps you understand the full story.

Primary CTA:

> Join the MiFlujo early-access waitlist.

Secondary CTAs:

- Follow the page for launch updates.
- Visit https://miflujo.taoforge.org to get early access.
- Direct business, lender, or audit use cases to https://miflujo.taoforge.org.

Launch offer:

> We are selecting 10 early users for free trial access before public launch.

Early users should give honest feedback, allow testimonial use where possible, and share what MiFlujo helped them discover from their bank statement.

## Positioning

MiFlujo turns Nigerian bank statements into financial intelligence. Users can upload PDF, CSV, or XLSX statements and get spending insights, transaction categorization, cashflow analysis, recurring payment detection, unusual transaction flags, reports, and AI-powered answers.

## Audience Segments

- Individuals who want deeper understanding of spending.
- Couples and families managing shared household expenses.
- SMEs and founders who need clearer cashflow visibility.
- Lending organizations that review bank statements.
- Auditing and accounting firms that analyze financial activity.
- Any team or entity with a tight relationship with bank statements.

## Words To Prefer

- Financial intelligence
- Spending insights
- Cashflow analysis
- Household budget clarity
- Unusual transaction detection
- Recurring payment detection
- Early access
- Free trial
- Social proof
- Testimonials

## Words To Avoid

- Social engineering
- Guaranteed fraud detection
- Guaranteed credit decision
- We can access your bank account
- We store your banking credentials

## Trust Language

Use:

> MiFlujo uses your bank statement to extract transactions, summarize cashflow, and generate spending insights. You can delete processed statement data from your account.

Use this shorter version when space is tight:

> Your statement is used to extract transactions and build your financial insights. You can delete processed data anytime.

## Recommended Monthly Flow

- Week 1: Curiosity and pain. Make people question what their statement already knows.
- Week 2: Education. Explain what statements reveal for users, SMEs, lenders, and auditors.
- Week 3: Product reveal. Show snippets of upload, dashboard, analytics, and AI assistant.
- Week 4: Countdown. Push the 10-user free trial, collect waitlist names, and prepare testimonials.

## Waitlist Intake Questions

Collect these through the early-access flow:

1. Name
2. Email or WhatsApp number
3. Are you an individual, business, lender, auditor, or other?
4. What do you want to understand from bank statements?
5. Can we contact you for feedback and a possible testimonial?
