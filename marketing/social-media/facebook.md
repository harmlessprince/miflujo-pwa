# Facebook Launch Playbook

## Role

Facebook is useful for community-style posts, SME groups, personal network updates, fintech groups, and longer educational posts.

Tone: friendly, explanatory, practical.

## Content Pillars

- Personal finance education.
- SME cashflow clarity.
- Manual statement analysis pain.
- Early access invitation.
- Product snippets and founder updates.

## Cadence

Post 3 times per week:

- 1 educational post.
- 1 product snippet.
- 1 early-access or community question post.
