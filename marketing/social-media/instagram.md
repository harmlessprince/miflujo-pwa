# Instagram Launch Playbook

## Role

Instagram should make MiFlujo feel useful, visual, and easy to understand. Focus on individuals, founders, freelancers, and SMEs.

Tone: direct, curious, modern, practical.

## Content Pillars

- Your statement knows more than your balance.
- Spending leaks and recurring payments.
- Before and after: raw statement to clear insight.
- Product snippets from upload, dashboard, analytics, and AI assistant.
- Early access for 10 free trial users.

## Cadence

Post 5 times per week:

- 2 reels
- 2 carousels
- 1 screenshot/product snippet
- Daily stories/status-style updates during final countdown week
