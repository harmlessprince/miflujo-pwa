# MiFlujo Real-Life Story Ideas

Use these scenarios as relatable marketing angles for MiFlujo. Each story should connect everyday money pressure to clearer financial intelligence from bank statements.

Primary CTA:

> Visit https://miflujo.taoforge.org to get early access.

## Story Ideas

### 1. Where Did My Salary Go?

A person gets paid, settles bills, sends money, buys food, pays subscriptions, and by mid-month the balance is already low.

MiFlujo helps them see the real spending pattern, not just the remaining balance.

### 2. Small Business Cashflow Confusion

A business owner is making sales but still feels broke.

MiFlujo shows income, expenses, recurring payments, supplier costs, and where cash is leaking.

### 3. Family Support / Black Tax Tracking

Someone regularly sends money to parents, siblings, emergencies, school fees, or medical needs.

MiFlujo helps them understand how much family support is affecting monthly cashflow without guilt.

### 4. Subscription And Recurring Payment Creep

A person forgets about small recurring payments such as streaming, cloud storage, data plans, loan apps, or app subscriptions.

MiFlujo surfaces repeated payments so they can decide what to keep or cancel.

### 5. Couples Planning A Major Expense

A couple wants to rent a new apartment, pay school fees, buy a car, or plan a wedding.

MiFlujo helps them understand their current spending before committing to a bigger financial obligation.

### 6. Lending Or Cooperative Contribution Story

A lender, ajo/esusu group, or cooperative needs to understand if someone's bank statement shows stable income and healthy cashflow.

MiFlujo helps review patterns faster.

### 7. Freelancer Income Instability

A freelancer earns irregularly. Some months are great, some are dry.

MiFlujo helps them see average income, peak months, low months, and spending habits so they can plan better.

### 8. School Fees Season Pressure

A parent feels financially squeezed when school fees, uniforms, books, transport, and feeding hit together.

MiFlujo helps show whether the pressure is seasonal or part of a larger spending trend.

### 9. Earning More But Still Not Saving

Someone gets a raise or better income, but savings do not improve.

MiFlujo can reveal lifestyle creep, increased transfers, bigger food or transport costs, and hidden recurring expenses.

### 10. Emergency Expenses Disrupting Everything

A medical bill, family emergency, repair, or travel cost throws off the month.

MiFlujo helps separate unusual one-off expenses from normal spending patterns.

### 11. Market Price Inflation

The same house budget no longer buys the same things because food, fuel, transport, and utilities have increased.

MiFlujo helps families see what changed and revise the budget intelligently.

### 12. Shared Apartment / Roommate Expenses

Flatmates split rent, light bill, internet, cleaning, repairs, and food.

MiFlujo helps one person track what is being paid repeatedly and what keeps causing shortfalls.

### 13. Loan Repayment Pressure

Someone has salary deductions, loan app repayments, buy-now-pay-later payments, or informal debt repayments.

MiFlujo helps reveal how much of their income is already committed before they spend.

### 14. Religious And Community Giving

Tithes, offerings, donations, community dues, and support contributions can be meaningful but hard to track.

MiFlujo helps users understand the percentage of income going there.

### 15. Mixing Personal And Business Money

A founder uses one account for business sales, supplier payments, family expenses, and personal spending.

MiFlujo helps separate the story and show what is really happening.

## Strongest Nigerian Relatability Angles

- Salary disappearing before month-end.
- Family support / black tax.
- School fees season pressure.
- Market price inflation.
- Small business cashflow confusion.
- Mixing personal and business money.

## Usage Notes

- Keep stories warm, practical, and non-judgmental.
- Focus on clarity and better decisions, not shame.
- Connect every story back to what a bank statement can reveal.
