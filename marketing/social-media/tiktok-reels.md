# TikTok And Reels Launch Playbook

## Role

TikTok and Reels should make the product feel obvious in under 30 seconds. Use simple money pain, quick product snippets, and clear early-access CTAs.

Tone: fast, visual, practical.

## Cadence

Post 3 to 5 short videos per week.

Ideal length:

- 7 to 12 seconds for teaser videos.
- 20 to 35 seconds for education.
- 30 to 45 seconds for product demo.

## Video Hooks

- You are checking your balance, but ignoring the real story.
- Your bank statement can expose where your money is leaking.
- Stop reading bank statements manually.
- This is what your statement looks like after MiFlujo analyzes it.
- If you run a business, your statement should not be a mystery.
- We are giving 10 people free access before launch.
