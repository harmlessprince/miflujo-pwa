# WhatsApp Launch Playbook

## Role

WhatsApp is for warm audience momentum: friends, founders, operators, finance contacts, small business owners, early testers, and referrals.

Tone: personal, direct, human.

## Cadence

- Status: 1 to 3 posts daily.
- Broadcast: 2 per week until launch.
- Final week: daily countdown status.

## Status Copy

### Status 1

Most people check their account balance.

But your bank statement tells the full story.

MiFlujo is coming this month.

### Status 2

We are selecting 10 people to try MiFlujo for free before launch.

If you want to understand your spending or analyze bank statements better, message me "MiFlujo".

### Status 3

MiFlujo helps turn bank statements into:

- spending insights
- cashflow analysis
- recurring payment detection
- unusual transaction flags
- AI-powered answers

Early access is open.

### Status 4

If you are a business, lender, auditor, accountant, or someone who works with bank statements, I want you in the early test group.

Message me "DEMO".

## Broadcast Message

Hi, I am preparing to launch MiFlujo at the end of this month.

MiFlujo helps people and businesses turn Nigerian bank statements into financial intelligence.

You can upload a statement and see spending insights, cashflow analysis, recurring payments, unusual transactions, and AI-powered answers.

I am selecting 10 early users for free trial access before public launch.

If you want to join, reply with:

1. Your name
2. Individual, business, lender, auditor, or other
3. What you want to understand from bank statements

## Personal DM Invite

Hey, I am launching MiFlujo this month.

It helps people upload bank statements and understand spending, cashflow, recurring payments, unusual transactions, and other financial patterns.

I am selecting 10 early users for free trial access and feedback. I thought this might be useful to you.

Would you like me to add you?

## Follow-Up After Interest

Great. Please send:

1. Your name
2. Email
3. Are you joining as an individual, business, lender, auditor, or other?
4. What do you want MiFlujo to help you understand?

I will add you to the early-access list.

## Testimonial Request

Thanks for trying MiFlujo.

Could you send a short honest note about your experience?

You can answer any of these:

- What did MiFlujo help you understand?
- What surprised you from your statement?
- Was it easier than reviewing the statement manually?
- Would you recommend it to someone else?

With your permission, we may use your feedback on the landing page or social media.
