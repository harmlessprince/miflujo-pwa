# WhatsApp Launch Playbook

## Role

WhatsApp is for warm audience momentum: friends, founders, operators, finance contacts, small business owners, early testers, and referrals.

Tone: personal, direct, human.

## Cadence

- Status: 1 to 3 posts daily.
- Broadcast: 2 per week until launch.
- Final week: daily countdown status.
